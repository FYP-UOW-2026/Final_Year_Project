# BioAudit Terms of Use

Version 2026-10. You accept these terms once, when you create an account. Each scan also
records an authorisation statement (target app, optional client name, date).

## 1. Test only what you are allowed to test
Use BioAudit only on apps you own, or apps whose owner has given you permission to test.
Do not use it against apps or devices belonging to anyone else. Runtime tests are gated
behind the authorisation confirmation (`--i-am-authorized` or the GUI checkbox); ticking it
is a statement that you hold that permission.

## 2. Where APKs come from
BioAudit does not download apps from any store. You supply the APK, either your own build
or one you are authorised to test. Do not use it to circumvent a store's or vendor's terms.

## 3. No exploitation
BioAudit demonstrates weaknesses to help fix them. Do not use findings to access data or
accounts you are not entitled to.

## 4. Your data
The APK and device data stay on your machine. Findings, with secrets redacted, are
uploaded to your account. A team admin can see their members' scans.

## 5. Responsibility
You are responsible for how you use the tool and for any permission you claim. The
authorisation record is stored with each scan as evidence of what you confirmed.

## 6. Changes
If these terms change, the version number changes and you will be asked to accept again.
